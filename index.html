Reviews

Employees receive performance reviews every quarter. The review score ranges from 1 to 5, based on performance. Reviews include manager feedback and improvement suggestions.

Working Hours

The company operates from Monday to Friday. Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Employees have a 1-hour break during working hours. Employees are expected to maintain punctuality and regular attendance.

Data Security

The company follows high-level data security practices. Employees must use secure passwords and authorized systems. Two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled for employee accounts. Sensitive company and customer data must remain confidential. Employees receive regular security awareness training.

Team Bonding

The team has been working together for approximately 3 years. The team currently has 8 members. The company conducts team activities and events throughout the year. Team collaboration and employee engagement are encouraged.
